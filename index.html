How the columns are built:
  • Online — MIS Daily rows where Collector ID matches the Online ID setting.
  • Cash Deposit to Bank — left blank in the downloaded Excel for you to fill in manually (the day-wise, TOTAL, and reconciliation formulas are already linked to it, so totals update as you enter values).
  • Easy Pay — every other collector ID's MIS Daily collection.
  • Total Collection — all MIS Daily payments for the month plus the matched Headwise charges.
  • Total Deposit — Cash Deposit to Bank + Online + Easy Pay.
The downloaded workbook has a single, formatted sheet with the final computed values — no raw data sheets are included.
