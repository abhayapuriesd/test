How the columns are built:
  • Online — MIS Daily rows where Collector ID matches the Online ID setting.
  • Cash Deposit to Bank — MIS Daily rows for the Cash-deposit collector IDs, plus Headwise rows whose Bill Head matches the configured disconnection/reconnection charges.
  • Easy Pay — every other collector ID's MIS Daily collection.
  • Total Collection — all MIS Daily payments for the month plus the matched Headwise charges.
  • Total Deposit — Cash Deposit to Bank + Online + Easy Pay.
The downloaded workbook also includes the raw uploaded data on two extra sheets, with the statement linked to them by formula — so it recalculates in Excel if you edit the raw rows.
